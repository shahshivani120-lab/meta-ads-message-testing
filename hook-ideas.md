# Hook ideas: 10 themes, 40 templates

A **theme** is the reason someone should reply (free, cheaper, faster, safer…). A **hook** is the first line that delivers it. Test themes first; once you know which theme wins, test hooks within it.

Replace the [brackets] with your category, numbers and offer. Examples are written for a restaurant POS (point-of-sale) SaaS, the business in the case study.

## How to pick themes to test

List every reason a buyer might switch to you, then group them. Good themes to test are:
- **Different from each other** (not three versions of "save money")
- **True and provable** (you can back the claim up in the chat)
- **Tied to an offer** someone can say yes to in a message

## The 10 themes

### 1. Free / no upfront cost
*Works when:* switching cost or upfront price is the main objection. **The case-study winner** (₹686 per chat).
1. "Still paying thousands for [category]? There's a better way."
2. "Get [hardware/setup] free when you switch this month."
3. "$0 upfront. Start using [product] this week."
4. "Switching shouldn't cost you anything. Here's how we make it free."

### 2. Named price
*Works when:* your price beats what they pay now. **Second in the case study** (₹958 per chat; the same idea without a price cost ₹2,136).
5. "[Tool A], [tool B] and [tool C] for [$X] a month. All in one."
6. "Still paying separately for [tool A], [tool B] and [tool C]? One price: [$X]."
7. "[$X] a month. No contracts. No hidden fees."
8. "What would you do with an extra [$Y] a month?"

### 3. Fees and percentages
*Works when:* buyers pay a percentage they rarely check. **Most-seen ad in the case study.**
9. "Still on [X]%? Your provider is making more from your business than you think."
10. "[X]% of every sale. Every day. It adds up to [$Y] a year."
11. "Check your last statement. What are you paying per transaction?"
12. "Lower fees, same day settlement. Want the numbers?"

### 4. Hidden costs and add-ons
*Works when:* competitors charge extra for features you include.
13. "Paying extra for [add-on 1]? [Add-on 2]? [Add-on 3]?"
14. "The base price isn't the real price. Here's what's usually added."
15. "Everything included: [feature 1], [feature 2], [feature 3]."
16. "How many subscriptions does your [business] pay for every month?"

### 5. Time saving
*Works when:* you can prove the time saved.
17. "[Task] in [short time] instead of [long time]."
18. "Close the day in 5 minutes, not 50."
19. "Stop [annoying task] by hand."
20. "Get [number] hours back every week."

### 6. Revenue / growth
*Works when:* the product helps them sell more.
21. "Your best customers come back [X]% more with [feature]."
22. "Turn [one-off visitors] into regulars."
23. "Which [menu items/products] actually make you money?"
24. "[Business type] using [feature] see [result]." (only with real data)

### 7. Comparison / switching
*Works when:* buyers already use a known competitor.
25. "Switching from [competitor]? Here's what changes (and what doesn't)."
26. "[Competitor] vs [you]: the honest comparison."
27. "We'll move your [data/menu/products] for you. Free."
28. "Thinking of leaving [competitor]? Ask us anything."

### 8. Local / specific audience
*Works when:* you can name the place or type of business.
29. "[City] [business type] owners: [offer]."
30. "Built for [specific type, e.g. cafés with 2–10 staff]."
31. "Now in [city]: [offer], with local support."
32. "[Number] [city] [businesses] switched this year. Here's why."

### 9. Seasonal / deadline
*Works when:* there's a real date. **The case study's Black Friday campaign used theme 1 with a deadline.**
33. "[Event] offer: [offer] until [date]."
34. "Get set up before [busy season]."
35. "Only [number] [setups/spots] left this month."
36. "Ends [date]: [offer]."

### 10. Pain (use with an offer, never alone)
*Works when:* paired with a clear offer. **On its own it was among the weakest in the case study** (₹2,611 per chat).
37. "Running a [business] already takes long hours. [Offer] gives you [benefit]."
38. "Wrong orders? Long queues? [Offer] fixes [specific problem]."
39. "Tired of [problem]? Try [offer], free for [time]."
40. "[Problem] costs you [amount]. [Offer] stops it."

## Rules of thumb

- **Offers start conversations; problems alone mostly get nods.**
- **A specific number beats a vague claim.** "$89" beats "affordable".
- **One theme per ad.** Don't stack "free + cheaper + faster".
- **The first line does the work.** Most people never read past it.
- **Make the reply easy:** end with a question ("Want the numbers?") and a pre-filled message.
