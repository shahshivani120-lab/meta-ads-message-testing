# Hook ideas by theme

Start with 5–8 themes. Write one hook per ad. Replace the [brackets] with your category and numbers.

| Theme | Hook pattern | Tends to work when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Free / no upfront cost** | "Still paying thousands for [category]? There's a better way." | Switching cost is the main objection |
| **Named price** | "[Tool A], [tool B] and [tool C] for [$X] a month, all in one." | Your price is a clear win vs the status quo |
| **Fees** | "Still on [X]%? Your provider is making more from your business than you think." | Buyers pay a percentage they rarely check |
| **Hidden costs** | "Paying extra for [add-on 1]? [Add-on 2]? [Add-on 3]?" | Competitors charge for add-ons separately |
| **Time saving** | "[Task] in [time] instead of [longer time]." | You can prove the time saved |
| **Comparison** | "Switching from [competitor]? Here's what changes." | Buyers already use a known competitor |
| **Pain only** | "Running a [business] already takes long hours…" | Rarely on its own: pair it with an offer |

## Rules of thumb

- One message per ad. If you can't say what the ad is testing, it's testing too much.
- A specific number beats a vague claim.
- Offers start conversations; problems alone mostly get nods.
- Change the creative before you change a winning offer.
