# WhatsApp and Messenger reply scripts

The ad's job is to start the conversation. **Your replies decide whether it becomes a customer.** Speed matters most: reply within 15 minutes in business hours.

Replace [brackets] with your details. Keep messages short: 1–3 lines each, like a person, not a brochure.

## 1. Automated messages (set once)

**Greeting message** (sent automatically when someone first messages)
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out to [Business] 👋 A real person will reply in a few minutes. To help us help you faster: what [POS / system] are you using now?

**Away message** (outside business hours)
> Thanks for your message! We're offline right now and back at [time] [time zone]. We'll reply first thing. In the meantime, here's a quick overview: [link]

## 2. First human reply (match it to the ad)

**Free / no upfront cost ad**
> Hi [Name], [your name] here from [Business]. Yes, the [hardware] is free when you switch this month. Can I ask 2 quick questions so I can tell you exactly what you'd get?

**Named price ad**
> Hi [Name]! The [$X] plan includes [A], [B] and [C], with no contracts. What are you paying for those today? Happy to do a quick side-by-side.

**Fees ad**
> Hi [Name]! Happy to compare. If you can share roughly what you process a month and your current %, I'll show you what you'd pay with us. No obligation.

## 3. Qualifying questions (ask 2–3, not all)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| What are you using now? | Shows switching effort and competitor |
| How many [locations / terminals / staff]? | Size and fit |
| What's the main thing you'd like to fix? | Their real pain |
| When are you looking to switch? | Urgency |
| Are you the one who decides, or someone else too? | Decision-maker |

## 4. Move to a call or demo

> Thanks, that's really helpful. Based on what you've said, I think [plan] fits. Easiest next step is a 15-minute call where I show you on screen. Does [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work?

Always offer **two specific times**, not "when are you free?"

## 5. Common objections

| They say | Reply |
|---|---|
| "Just send me the price." | "Sure: [plan] is [$X]/month including [A, B, C]. To make sure that's the right plan, how many [terminals] do you need?" |
| "I'm locked into a contract." | "Understood. When does it end? We can plan the switch for then, and sometimes we can help with exit costs." |
| "Switching sounds like a lot of work." | "We do the setup for you: [menu import, training, etc.]. Most [businesses] are running within [X days]." |
| "I'll think about it." | "Of course. Can I send a quick summary you can look at later? And is it OK if I check in on [day]?" |
| "Too expensive." | "Compared to what you pay now, or overall? Happy to do a line-by-line comparison with your current bill." |

## 6. Follow-up sequence (if they go quiet)

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | "Hi [Name], just checking you saw my last message about [offer]. Any questions I can answer?" |
| Day 3 | "Quick one: the [offer] is open until [date]. Want me to hold a spot for you?" |
| Day 7 | "I'll leave it here so I don't fill your inbox. If timing's better later, just reply here anytime. 🙂" |

Stop after three follow-ups. Respect "no" immediately.

## 7. Log every conversation

For each chat, log in the workbook or your CRM: **date, ad/test ID, theme, qualified (Y/N), call booked (Y/N), customer (Y/N), value.** This is how you move from cost per conversation to **cost per customer**.
